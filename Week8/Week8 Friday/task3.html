Validation Attributes

HTML can check required values, text lengths, patterns, and numeric ranges before a form is submitted.

Try submitting values outside the allowed ranges.

Validation attributes

required
pattern="[A-Za-z0-9]{3,12}"
min="18" max="100"
minlength="10" maxlength="120"
