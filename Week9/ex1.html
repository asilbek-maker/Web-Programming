Week 9 · Exercise 1

Accessibility Audit

Use Lighthouse to find barriers on your course project.

Steps

  1. Open your personal or team project in Chrome.
  2. Open Developer Tools, choose Lighthouse, and select the Accessibility category.
  3. Run the audit and save a screenshot of the initial score.
  4. Review the report and write down at least three issues to fix.

Quick checks

  • Can you use every link and control with the keyboard?
  • Does every form control have a connected label?
  • Do informative images have useful alternative text?
  • Is text easy to read against its background?
