How to Make Uzbek Plov

What You Need

  • Rice
  • Beef or lamb
  • Carrots
  • Onions
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt and spices

Steps

  1. Wash and prepare the rice.
  2. Cut the meat into small pieces.
  3. Heat the oil in a large pot.
  4. Be careful: the hot oil can splash.
  5. Add the meat and cook until brown.
  6. Add onions and carrots and cook for several minutes.
  7. Add water, salt, and spices.
  8. Add the rice and cook until the rice is soft.
  9. Serve the Uzbek plov hot and enjoy your meal.
